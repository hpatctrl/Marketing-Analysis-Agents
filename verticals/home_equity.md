# Home equity and mortgage

**How it makes money:** Interest on drawn balances; property is collateral.

## KPIs the team reports
- Combined LTV mix
- Draw rate
- Delinquency by CLTV
- Prepayment
- Loss severity

## Public data
- Freddie Mac single-family loan-level data
- HMDA

## Rules to check (with compliance-reviewer)
- TILA-RESPA, HMDA reporting, ECOA
- Line freeze and reduction notice rules

All economics assumptions for a case live in that case's `assumptions.yaml` or source file, labeled as assumptions.
