# Credit cards and co-brand

**How it makes money:** Interchange, interest on revolving balances, fees; rewards and partner payments are the big costs.

## KPIs the team reports
- Approval rate by channel
- Activation within 60 days
- Revolve rate
- Net charge-off rate
- Rewards cost / spend
- NPV per account

## Public data
- Amex default prediction (Kaggle)
- Fed G.19 consumer credit
- CFPB card agreement database

## Rules to check (with compliance-reviewer)
- Reg Z / CARD Act (ability to pay, under-21 rules, penalty fees)
- FCRA prescreen firm offers
- Partner contract: credit policy control and data rights

All economics assumptions for a case live in that case's `assumptions.yaml` or source file, labeled as assumptions.
