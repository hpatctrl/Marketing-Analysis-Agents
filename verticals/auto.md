# Auto loans

**How it makes money:** Interest over term; recoveries from repossession offset losses.

## KPIs the team reports
- Approval and look-to-book by dealer
- LTV and PTI mix
- Early payment default by dealer
- Cumulative net loss
- Recovery rate

## Public data
- SEC Reg AB II auto ABS loan-level data (Form ABS-EE)

## Rules to check (with compliance-reviewer)
- Dealer markup and fair lending
- Repossession and state notice rules

All economics assumptions for a case live in that case's `assumptions.yaml` or source file, labeled as assumptions.
