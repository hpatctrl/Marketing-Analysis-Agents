# Unsecured installment loans

**How it makes money:** Interest and origination fees over a fixed term; prepayment shortens revenue.

## KPIs the team reports
- Approval rate
- Booked APR by grade
- Early payment default
- Cumulative net loss by vintage
- Prepayment rate
- NPV per loan

## Public data
- LendingClub loan data
- SEC filings of public lenders

## Rules to check (with compliance-reviewer)
- State rate caps and licensing
- MLA 36% MAPR for covered borrowers
- Adverse-action notices

All economics assumptions for a case live in that case's `assumptions.yaml` or source file, labeled as assumptions.
