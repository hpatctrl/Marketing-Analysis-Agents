# Cash advance and earned wage access

**How it makes money:** Subscription or express fees, tips where allowed; losses are repayment failures.

## KPIs the team reports
- Approval rate
- Repayment rate at due date
- Advances per member per month
- Net loss % of originations
- ARPU
- Payback months

## Public data
- Simulated bank-transaction data (see Cash-Advance-Underwriting repo)

## Rules to check (with compliance-reviewer)
- State EWA laws
- Whether fees or tips make the product credit (TILA)
- UDAAP in fee and tip design

All economics assumptions for a case live in that case's `assumptions.yaml` or source file, labeled as assumptions.
