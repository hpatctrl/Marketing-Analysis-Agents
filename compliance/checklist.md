# Compliance and fair-lending checklist (first pass)

This checklist supports a first-pass review by the `compliance-reviewer` agent. It is not legal advice and does not replace review by qualified counsel or a compliance officer before anything goes live.

## Marketing and advertising
- [ ] **Credit-term triggers (Reg Z / TILA):** if an ad states a payment amount, number of payments, finance charge or down payment, the required APR and terms disclosures appear with it.
- [ ] **Prescreened offers (FCRA):** prescreen criteria are set before the pull; offers are firm offers of credit; the opt-out notice is included and clear.
- [ ] **UDAAP:** no claims about approval odds, savings or "no impact" that the product can't support; fees and rewards conditions are clear and near the claim.
- [ ] **Channel rules:** TCPA consent for texts and autodialed calls; CAN-SPAM for email; state telemarketing rules.
- [ ] **Digital targeting:** audience definitions and lookalike models reviewed for proxies of protected classes (geography, language, interests).

## Credit decisions
- [ ] **ECOA / Reg B:** no prohibited bases used; model features reviewed for proxies; adverse-action notices with specific principal reasons.
- [ ] **Disparate-impact testing:** approval and pricing outcomes compared across groups; less discriminatory alternatives searched when gaps appear.
- [ ] **Ability to pay (cards, CARD Act)** and state rate caps and licensing for the product.
- [ ] **Military Lending Act** coverage checks and 36% MAPR where it applies.
- [ ] **Model risk management:** documentation, independent validation, monitoring plan (SR 11-7 style).

## Product and servicing
- [ ] Fee design reviewed for UDAAP risk (late, overdraft-like, express or tip mechanics).
- [ ] Collections practices (FDCPA where it applies, state rules); hardship options.
- [ ] Partner and bank-partner contracts: who controls credit policy, complaints, data and marketing approval.
- [ ] Complaints monitoring with categories mapped to products and channels.
